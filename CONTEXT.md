# Pool plugin domain vocabulary

## Natural water feature

A naturalistic secondary water system associated with a swimming pool. It is
made of a lower catch basin, a connecting watercourse, a waterfall or
spillway, and surrounding rockwork.

## Lower catch basin

The smaller lower body of water that receives water from the main pool through
the watercourse or spillway. It is not a spa unless it has intentional seating,
jets, and heated-water behavior.

## Watercourse

The visible channel or stream bed carrying water between two basins.

## Waterfall / spillway

The elevated transition where water leaves the upper pool or watercourse and
falls into the lower catch basin. “Spillway” refers to the controlled overflow
edge; “waterfall” refers to the visible falling-water effect.

## Rockwork

The boulders, retaining stones, ledges, and rock surround that shape and hide
the natural water feature.
