# Pool skimmer model

## Real-world behavior

A residential pool skimmer is a recessed, wall-mounted surface-water intake. The pump pulls water through a hinged floating weir. Floating leaves and insects pass over the weir into a removable basket; filtered water continues through the suction line to the pump. The deck lid provides access for basket cleaning, and many products also provide an optional overflow/equalizer connection or vacuum plate.

The mouth is installed at the finished design waterline, normally with the water level around the middle of the throat. The skimmer should face the prevailing wind/downwind edge so wind carries debris toward it. Return jets should support a gentle circulation loop toward the skimmer rather than discharge directly into its mouth.

## Model assumptions

- Local `+Z` is the pool-facing direction; the housing and suction port extend toward `-Z`.
- The origin is the center of the mouth at the design waterline.
- Default body dimensions are a representative residential skimmer, not a manufacturer-specific installation template.
- The model exposes the mouth, floating weir, basket, access lid, and suction port so the item reads correctly in a cutaway/3D scene.
- Final construction needs to be checked against the selected skimmer manufacturer, pool shell type, local plumbing rules, and structural details.
